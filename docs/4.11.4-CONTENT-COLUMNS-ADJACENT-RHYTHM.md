# 4.11.4 — Content Columns adjacent rhythm

## Problem

Two consecutive service Content Columns each carried `section-pad`. At desktop this produced `110px` bottom padding plus another `110px` top padding at the shared boundary, visually creating a ~220px content-to-content gap.

## Resolution

When two service-theme Content Columns are adjacent, the single global section rhythm is split evenly:

- preceding section bottom: `calc(var(--section-y) / 2)`
- following section top: `calc(var(--section-y) / 2)`

The total boundary remains exactly one `--section-y`. Standalone sections retain their full normal padding. Paper and White continue to share the same typography and internal geometry.
